Reminders need Chrome running. If MyLS is unavailable, they use your saved dates. “Morning of” means 8 AM, or one hour before an earlier deadline.

Sync details & privacy

Dates use your device’s time zone. Local check-offs never submit coursework. MyLS completion is shown only when reported by MyLS. Pearson, Achieve and Top Hat read loaded coursework in supported open tabs after you connect them. Their coverage can be partial. When a platform item matches a MyLS item, it shows once under the platform, and the matching details are listed below.

Course data is cached in this browser. When you add a deadline to Google Calendar, its title, course, due date and source link are sent to Google. No analytics or MYLD backend.
